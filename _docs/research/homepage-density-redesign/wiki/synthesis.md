# Synthesis

## Preserved thesis

- [INFERENCE anonymous-homepage-render,anonymous-homepage-template] Reducing homepage density requires fewer competing destinations and fewer repeated card grids, not merely smaller padding.
- [INFERENCE anonymous-homepage-render,anonymous-homepage-template] A calmer public-page story is: promise, three benefits, three participation paths, current activity, one proof point, membership, and one final action.
- [HUMAN] Book Club belongs beside Community Sprints and Live Events as a first-class way to participate. It should not become another long standalone homepage section.
- [INFERENCE anonymous-homepage-template] Blog and Workshops remain discoverable through navigation and footer links without each consuming a full homepage preview band.

## Preserved design direction

1. Replace the Philosophy and activities card walls with three roomy benefit pillars.
2. Introduce a single `Find your way in` section containing Community Sprints, Live Events, and Book Club.
3. Describe Book Club as `Read together, one chapter at a time`, with shared notes and weekly discussions.
4. Compress live content into one `What's happening now` band.
5. Use one strong testimonial and a short supporting metric row instead of a testimonial grid.
6. Emphasize Main membership while keeping Basic and Premium as quieter comparison choices.
7. End with one calm signup action and a compact footer.

## Status boundary

- [HUMAN] This direction is liked and should be retained.
- [OPEN] It has not been approved for anonymous-homepage implementation.
- [OPEN] The authenticated dashboard needs a separate audit and concept based on its member tasks, progress, events, sprint plan, and Book Club state.

## Authenticated-dashboard thesis

- [INFERENCE logged-in-dashboard-render,logged-in-dashboard-template] The member dashboard should lead with the most consequential next task, not with two peer columns and a six-card directory.
- [INFERENCE logged-in-dashboard-template] Sprint-plan progress is the strongest candidate for the dominant action when a member has an active plan; its current conditional placement below Quick actions weakens that priority.
- [INFERENCE logged-in-dashboard-render,logged-in-dashboard-template] Courses, workshops, and Book Club are continuity surfaces and belong together under `Continue`, with progress and a single next action.
- [HUMAN] Book Club must be visible to logged-in members as an active reading-progress module, not merely as a footer link or generic navigation shortcut.
- [INFERENCE logged-in-dashboard-render] Events, Slack, polls, discovery links, and recent content remain useful but should be progressively quieter than the member's sprint and learning work.

## Authenticated concept direction

1. Lead with `Your next step` and one prominent sprint-plan CTA.
2. Put course and Book Club progress together in a wider `Continue` column.
3. Put only the next two scheduled items in a smaller `Up next` rail.
4. Compress poll, Slack, and sprint/cohort discovery into one Community strip.
5. Reduce Recent content to three editorial rows.
6. Replace six descriptive Quick action cards with one compact Explore icon row.
7. Keep the authenticated page around one to one-and-a-half desktop viewports where the available state permits.
