Merge accounts

Consolidate a duplicate (secondary) account into a surviving (canonical) one. Eligible owned rows are repointed, secondary API credentials are revoked, profile/entitlement fields reconcile by precedence, the secondary email becomes an alias of the canonical account, and the secondary login is deactivated. This is irreversible — always review the dry-run preview before confirming.
